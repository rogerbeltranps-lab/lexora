# Third-party dictionary data

Lexora's original 36-entry starter glossary is separate from any imported Wiktionary data.

Additional dictionary entries, when imported using `scripts/import_kaikki.py`, come from **Wiktionary**, processed and distributed by **Kaikki / Wiktextract** (Tatu Ylonen).

- Source: https://kaikki.org/dictionary/
- Original content: https://en.wiktionary.org/

- CC BY-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- Extractor: https://github.com/tatuylonen/wiktextract

Wiktionary-sourced dictionary files are subject to their source licenses and attribution requirements, independently of the Lexora application copyright © Roger Beltran. Do not label Wiktionary-derived definitions as originally authored by Roger Beltran. Maintain this attribution when redistributing or adapting those files.

Kaikki data is regularly regenerated, so keep a note of the source dump date whenever importing a dataset.
