My Lexora

Lexora retrieves small dictionary files from GitHub Pages. Your personal cards, review history, imported EPUBs and reading preferences are stored in this browser. With Google Sign-In, cards and review progress can synchronize across devices.

Appearance

Choose Light or Dark, or automatically follow your computer’s appearance. Applies to all of Lexora, including the reader.

Pronunciation & Audio

On a computer, Lexora reads with the Kokoro natural voices (Heart unless you choose another). The first time, the voice model downloads once (about 100–300 MB) and the browser keeps it. Your computer’s built-in voices are still in the list.

Data backup

The JSON backup includes vocabulary and progress, not EPUB book files. Signed in with Google, your EPUBs are kept in your cloud library instead.

Manage your cards

Add or edit your own terms. New vocabulary from ChatGPT will be automatically available across devices only after an explicitly authorized synchronization integration is built.
