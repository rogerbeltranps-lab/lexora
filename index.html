Pronunciation & Audio

In Read & Listen, choose Natural voice · Kokoro for enhanced American English narration, or Device voice for the built-in option. Kokoro downloads its model on first use and synthesizes speech on your device; loading may be slow on phones. The voice list above applies to Device voice.

Data backup

The JSON backup includes vocabulary and progress, not EPUB book files. Reimport EPUBs separately when switching devices.

Manage your cards

Add or edit your own terms. New vocabulary from ChatGPT will be automatically available across devices only after an explicitly authorized synchronization integration is built.
